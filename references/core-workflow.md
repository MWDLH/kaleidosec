# Core Engagement Workflow

Use this workflow for every domain. Repeat stages when evidence changes the operating context.

## 1. Initialize

Establish objective, target class, scope status, time constraints, exclusions, available access, and proof threshold. Resume matching state or initialize a new state. Inventory tools and local analysis material without assuming a platform.

## 2. Inspect Current Capability

Record identity, privilege, application role, host or device, network position, execution channel, reachable boundaries, controls, and limitations. A capability change triggers focused recollection.

## 3. Collect Information

Collect assets and entry points; reachability and trust boundaries; technologies, versions, and deployment context; identities, roles, and authorization relationships; data flows and business value; configurations and controls; and current access and constraints.

Prefer passive and low-impact methods first, then targeted active enumeration. Preserve source and timestamp for decision-changing observations.

## 4. Check Coverage

Before exploitation, verify that major visible surfaces, identity boundaries, technology context, and important unknowns are represented. Coverage is sufficient when the next action is evidence-supported and missing information is unlikely to make it random or misdirected.

Do not confuse completeness with exhaustive enumeration. Record unreachable or deferred surfaces.

## 5. Model Attack Surface

Convert observations into components, identities, trust edges, data stores, management planes, entry points, and controls. Identify actions that would change privilege, reachability, identity, or business impact.

## 6. Generate And Rank Paths

Create testable paths rather than isolated labels. Include objective contribution, prerequisites, supporting and opposing evidence, expected gain, cost, risk, and falsifiers.

## 7. Validate And Execute

Choose the smallest action that distinguishes the leading hypothesis from alternatives. Check scope and the global safety gate. Execute and capture exact observations without assuming the expected result occurred.

## 8. Interpret And Replan

Classify results as confirmed, contradicted, partial, ambiguous, or execution failure. Update evidence and path status. Recollect when identity, privilege, reachability, trust, or environment changes.

## 9. Prove Impact

Use the minimum evidence needed to establish real impact. Prefer bounded proof of reachability, authorization failure, controlled access, or data exposure over unnecessary control expansion.

## 10. Review And Close

Review active, parked, and closed paths; document untested areas; reconcile changes; clean test artifacts; preserve evidence; and produce the report and handoff.
