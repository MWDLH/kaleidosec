# Engagement Report Template

## Executive Summary

State objective, tested scope, achieved proof, key attack chains, material business impact, and major limitations in language appropriate to the audience.

## Scope And Method

Record assets, identities or roles, environments, exclusions, time window, access supplied, test depth, and methods used. Separate tested, partially tested, unreachable, and deferred surfaces.

## Attack Surface And Coverage

Summarize discovered assets, identities, trust boundaries, management planes, data flows, and coverage gaps.

## Attack Chains

For each chain, show current access, evidence-backed steps, prerequisite at each step, final impact, confidence, and where the chain was stopped. Mark unverified links as hypotheses.

## Findings

For each finding include identifier, title, severity and rationale, affected assets, prerequisites, evidence, reproduction at an appropriate level, impact, chain relationships, detection considerations, remediation, and retest method.

## Parked And Rejected Paths

Record high-value paths not completed, why they were parked, reactivation conditions, and important hypotheses disproved during testing.

## Change And Cleanup Ledger

List every persistent change, creator action, affected asset, rollback instruction, rollback result, retained artifact, and owner for unresolved cleanup.

## Handoff

Provide current state, remaining blockers, recommended next actions, sensitive artifact locations, and final state checkpoint reference.
